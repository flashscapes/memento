These files are only for trying Memento before using your own photos. They are low resolution (640 px or smaller on the long edge), so they look softer than phone photos will.

## Music (public/audio)

`gentle.mp3` and `warm.mp3` are original instrumentals synthesized by `tools/make_music.py` in this repository (FM electric piano, soft pads, algorithmic reverb). No samples or third-party recordings are used. They are dedicated to the public domain (CC0) by the project owner.
