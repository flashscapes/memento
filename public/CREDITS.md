# Music credits

The twelve soundtracks in `public/audio` are original instrumentals synthesized by `tools/make_music.py` in this repository (FM electric piano, pads, plucked strings, drums, algorithmic reverb). No samples or third-party recordings are used. They are dedicated to the public domain (CC0) by the project owner.

Memento ships no sample photos or videos. Photos and clips you choose stay on your device until you export an MP4 to a renderer you run yourself.
